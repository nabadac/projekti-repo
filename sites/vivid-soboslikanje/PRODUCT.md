## Users

Two audiences, equally important (confirmed):
- Private clients in Zagreb and surroundings: owners of flats and houses doing adaptations, new builds or a refresh. They want a clean, reliable crew, a fair price and kept deadlines.
- Business clients: shops, petrol stations, retail chains, venues, offices. They need work done fast, tidy, inside an operating space, often under time pressure.

Job on the site: decide this crew is trustworthy, see proof of real work, then call or request a free quote.

## Product Purpose

Vivid soboslikanje (legal entity Forever Green d.o.o., Radnička cesta 27, 10000 Zagreb) is a family-founded painting company. Services: machine (airless) and hand painting, gletanje (skim coating / wall repair), knauf drywall (partition walls, suspended ceilings, cladding) by licensed drywall fitters, decorative techniques. The site exists to win new projects: phone calls and quote requests.

## Positioning

Family firm that "never found average good enough": professional machine-spraying equipment plus a crew that answers every call, keeps appointments to the minute and checks colours with the client throughout. Offers are individually tailored. Proven on live commercial interiors (petrol-station shops, retail) where work happens around open trading.
